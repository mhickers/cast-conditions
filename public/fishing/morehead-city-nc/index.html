Morehead City, NC fishing report

Morehead City fishes Bogue Sound and the nearshore reefs along North Carolina's Crystal Coast, a productive crossroads of inshore and nearshore fishing. The sound and its marshes hold red drum, speckled trout, and flounder through much of the year, with drum and trout feeding hardest in the cooler shoulder seasons. The nearshore reefs and wrecks give up black sea bass, sheepshead, and seasonal pelagics like Spanish and king mackerel that push close to the beach when the water warms.

In the sound, work the marsh edges, oyster bars, and channel drops with soft plastics and topwater for trout and redfish, and drift the deeper channels with bucktails or live bait for flounder. On the nearshore reefs, drop bait and jigs to the structure for sea bass and sheepshead, and troll or cast for mackerel when they're around. Tide and water temperature drive the inshore bite — fish the moving water around oyster structure and creek mouths at low light.
