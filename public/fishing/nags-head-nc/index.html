Nags Head, NC fishing report

Nags Head fishes the Outer Banks surf and the sounds behind the barrier islands, where the warm Gulf Stream's influence meets cooler northern water and produces a remarkable species mix. Red drum and striped bass work the surf, with the fall run a highlight as bait pours down the beach. Bluefish blitz through chasing it, and flounder fill the sound channels and inlets through the warm months. The sounds also hold speckled trout and the occasional bonus fish.

In the surf, fish the cuts and sloughs on a moving tide — fresh bait on a fishfinder rig for drum, metal and swim shads when blues and stripers blitz. In the sounds, drift the channel edges and around the inlets with bucktails or soft plastics for flounder and work soft plastics for speckled trout in the shallows and creek mouths. The surf and sounds both fish best on moving water; concentrate on the tide change and the first and last hours of light.
