Hilton Head Island, SC fishing report

Hilton Head fishes the sounds and the maze of tidal creeks that thread its salt marsh, prime Lowcountry inshore water. Red drum are the year-round headliner, schooling in the creeks and tailing on the flooded flats, with spotted seatrout filling the creeks and grass edges and flounder holding around the channel mouths and structure. Sheepshead stack on the dock pilings and hard structure, and the big tidal swings constantly reshape where and how the fish feed.

Fish the oyster bars, grass lines, and creek mouths with soft plastics, topwater, and live shrimp for redfish and trout, working the flooded grass on the high tide when reds tail for crabs. Drift the channel edges with bucktails or live bait for flounder, and fish fiddler crabs tight to pilings for sheepshead. The large tidal range is the key dial — the bite turns on with the moving water that floods and drains the marsh, especially around the tide change and at low light.
