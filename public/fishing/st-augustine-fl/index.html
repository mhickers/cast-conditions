St. Augustine, FL fishing report

St. Augustine fishes its inlet, the Intracoastal Waterway, and an open surf, a northeast Florida crossroads with strong year-round inshore fishing. Red drum and speckled trout are the staples, working the flats, creeks, and ICW through every season, with bull reds gathering around the inlet in fall. Flounder hold along the structure and channel edges, sheepshead stack on the bridges and dock pilings, and the surf and nearshore water give up whiting, pompano, and seasonal pelagics.

Work the flats, creek mouths, and ICW structure with soft plastics, topwater, and live bait for redfish and trout, timing the moving water around the inlet and bridges. Big reds want cut or live bait on the bottom near the inlet in fall, and flounder come on drifted bucktails along the channel edges. In the surf, fish bait on a fishfinder rig for pompano and whiting. The inlet and ICW key on current — fish the moving tide and the low-light windows.
