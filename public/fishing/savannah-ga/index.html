Savannah, GA fishing report

Savannah fishes the tidal rivers and sounds where the salt marsh meets the Georgia coast, a big-tide estuary with strong inshore fishing. Red drum are the foundation, schooling in the creeks and on the flats through every season, with spotted seatrout filling the grass edges and creek mouths and flounder holding around the structure and channels. Sheepshead stack on the pilings and hard structure, and the large tidal range drives where the fish feed as much as the calendar does.

Work the oyster bars, grass edges, and creek mouths with soft plastics, topwater, and live bait for redfish and trout, fishing the flooded grass on high water when reds tail. Drift the channel edges with bucktails or live bait for flounder, and fish fiddler crabs tight to pilings for sheepshead. The big tides are the master dial — the bite fires on the moving water that floods and drains the marsh, with the tide change and low-light windows the most productive.
