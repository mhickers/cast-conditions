Frenchtown, NJ fishing report

Frenchtown fishes the upper Delaware River, a clean, free-flowing stretch that's some of the best smallmouth bass water in the region. Smallmouth dominate, holding in the riffles, ledges, and current breaks and feeding aggressively from late spring through fall. The upper river also holds walleye in the faster runs and deeper pools, channel catfish in the holes, and panfish along the slower edges, with the occasional musky for those targeting them.

Work the riffles, rocky ledges, and eddies behind current breaks for smallmouth with tubes, grubs, crankbaits, and topwater early and late — the seam where fast water meets slow is where bass wait to ambush. Walleye hold in deeper runs; work jigs and crankbaits through them at low light. The upper river is flow- and temperature-driven: stable, moderate flow with warm water means active fish in the current, while high muddy water after rain pushes them to soft edges.
