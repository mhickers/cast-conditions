Charleston, SC fishing report

Charleston fishes its harbor, a vast network of tidal creeks, and the nearshore reefs, classic Lowcountry water with year-round opportunity. Red drum are the foundation, schooling in the creeks and on the flats and feeding through every season; spotted seatrout fill the creeks and grass edges, flounder hold around the structure and channel mouths, and sheepshead stack on the dock pilings, jetties, and reefs. The fishery shifts with the tide as much as the season in this big-tidal-range estuary.

Work the oyster bars, grass edges, and creek mouths with soft plastics, topwater, and live bait for redfish and trout, timing the flats for flooding tides when reds tail in the grass. Flounder come on bucktails and live bait drifted along the channel edges. Sheepshead want fiddler crabs fished tight to pilings and structure. The big tides drive everything — fish the moving water as it floods and drains the creeks and flats, with the low-light windows the most productive.
