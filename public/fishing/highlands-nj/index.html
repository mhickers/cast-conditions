Highlands, NJ fishing report

Highlands fishes Sandy Hook, the rips, and the bay where the Shrewsbury and Navesink meet Raritan Bay, prime striped bass country at the doorstep of New York Harbor. Spring brings a strong run of bass feeding on bunker in the bay and rips, and the fall run repeats it as fish push south. Summer fills the bay channels and flats with fluke, and bluefish blitz through chasing bait, with the sheltered water fishing well when the ocean is blown out.

Fish the rips off the hook and the bay channels with bucktails, swim shads, and soft plastics for stripers, live-lining bunker where the bait schools up. Drift bucktails along the channel edges for fluke. The rips fire on a hard moving tide and go slack at the turn, so time your drifts to the current. The bass bite is best on moving water at dawn and dusk; when bunker are thick, that's where the biggest fish will be.
