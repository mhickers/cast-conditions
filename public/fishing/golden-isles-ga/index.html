Golden Isles, GA fishing report

The Golden Isles offer year-round action across an ever-changing seasonal rotation. Striped bass push into the sounds and tidal creeks during cooler months, while weakfish work the same corridors in spring before the heat drives them off. Summer brings fluke settling onto sandy bottom transitions near inlet mouths, and bluefish slashing through schools of baitfish along the beachfront. Kingfish move into the surf in warmer months, making them a reliable target from the beach. Black sea bass and tautog tend to hold on nearshore structure when water temperatures allow, though both species thin out considerably during the hottest stretches.

Tides are everything here. Falling water pulls bait out of the marsh grass and concentrates fish along deeper creek edges and at the mouths where creeks dump into larger sounds. Position yourself at those transitions during the last two hours of outgoing tide for the most reliable action. Fluke respond well to drifting gulp-style baits or live finger mullet along sandy bottom transitions. Stripers and weakfish favor suspending plugs or soft plastics worked slowly in lower light. Bluefish will hit nearly anything metal or fast-moving in the surf. When water temperatures spike into the upper eighties, push toward deeper sound water or fish early morning before the heat kills the bite.
