Chatham, MA fishing report

Chatham fishes the Cape Cod flats and the powerful rips that form over the shoals off its elbow, prime striped bass and bluefish country. Stripers arrive in spring and feed through fall, prowling the flats and stacking in the rips where current and shoals concentrate bait; the fall run is a highlight as bass and blues blitz before heading south. Summer fills the deeper channels and structure with fluke and black sea bass, and bluefish blitz through chasing bait.

On the flats, sight-cast and work soft plastics and flies to cruising stripers on a moving tide; in the rips, swing bucktails, swim shads, and live bait through the current seams for bass and blues. Drift bucktails for fluke over the deeper structure, and fish bait or jigs on the bottom for sea bass. The rips and flats both key on current — the bite fires on moving water and slacks off at the turn, so time it to the tide and the low-light windows.
