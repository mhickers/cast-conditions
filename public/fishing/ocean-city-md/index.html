Ocean City, MD fishing report

Ocean City, Maryland fishes its inlet, an open surf, and the back bays behind it, a versatile mid-Atlantic fishery. The inlet is the centerpiece — striped bass run through it in spring and fall, flounder fill it and the bay channels through summer, and tautog stack on the inlet rocks and nearby structure into the cold months. Bluefish blitz through chasing bait, and the surf produces kingfish in summer and a strong bass and blues bite during the fall run.

Fish the inlet on moving water with bucktails, swim shads, and live bait for stripers, and drift bucktails or a squid-and-minnow combo along the channel edges for flounder. Tog want crab and clam fished tight to the rocks. In the surf, fish the cuts on a moving tide with bait on a fishfinder rig, switching to metal when blues blitz. The inlet bite is current-driven — the strongest action comes on a hard moving tide an hour either side of the turn, at dawn and dusk.
