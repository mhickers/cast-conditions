Boca Grande, FL fishing report

Boca Grande fishes the famous pass and Charlotte Harbor behind it, a southwest Florida legend best known as one of the world's premier tarpon destinations. Giant tarpon stack in the pass and along the beaches in their late-spring and summer migration, drawing anglers from around the globe. Snook hold around the pass, beaches, and mangrove structure through the warm months, redfish school on the harbor flats and oyster bars, and grouper and snapper hold on the nearshore structure.

In the pass, fish live bait and heavy jigs deep in the current for migrating tarpon, timing the strong tidal flow. Work the beaches and mangrove edges with live bait, swimbaits, and topwater for snook, and fish the harbor flats and oyster bars with soft plastics and live bait for redfish, especially on the flooding tide. The pass is all about current — the tarpon bite peaks on the strong moving tides, while snook and reds feed best on moving water at low light.
