Sea Isle City, NJ fishing report

Sea Isle City fishes Ludlam Bay behind it and an open beachfront in front, a quieter stretch of the South Jersey shore with a solid mixed-bag fishery. Summer is fluke season across the bay's channels and along the beach, with kingfish in the suds and weakfish on the bay rips. Striped bass and bluefish work the surf during the spring and fall runs, with the fall blitz the standout as bait pours down the beach.

In the bay, drift the channel edges and rips with bucktails or a squid-and-minnow combo for fluke and work soft plastics at dusk for weakfish. Along the beach, fish the cuts and troughs on a moving tide — bait on a fishfinder rig for the bottom, metal and swim shads when bass and blues are crashing. As on the rest of this coast, the tide is the key: concentrate on moving water an hour either side of the turn, with low light the best of all.
